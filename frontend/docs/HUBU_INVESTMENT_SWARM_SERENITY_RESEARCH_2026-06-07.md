## Serenity Pattern: Chokepoint Investing

The repeated pattern in public writeups:

1. Start from a fast-growing downstream theme:
   - AI data centers;
   - optical interconnect;
   - silicon photonics;
   - robotics / edge AI;
   - compute infrastructure.
2. Walk upstream through the supply chain:
   - materials;
   - substrate;
   - lasers;
   - packaging;
   - test equipment;
   - niche component suppliers.
3. Look for the narrow bottleneck:
   - few suppliers;
   - high technical barrier;
   - sudden demand inflection;
   - market cap still small;
   - public filings already hint at demand or customer pull.
4. Buy before the broad narrative becomes obvious.
5. Publish a thesis thread or long-form post.
6. Track whether company disclosures and industry events confirm or break the
   thesis.

This is closer to industrial intelligence plus asymmetric speculation than
classical value investing.

## Case Library

### AXTI

Reported Serenity thesis:

- AXT is an upstream substrate/material supplier exposed to AI optical
  communication demand.
- Chinese writeups describe AXTI as a high-multiple winner, with some articles
  saying Serenity entered near the low single digits.

Evidence to verify before Hubu accepts similar cases:

- issuer filings and investor decks;
- customer concentration;
- revenue mix from indium phosphide / gallium arsenide / germanium substrates;
- AI optical communication exposure vs legacy telecom exposure;
- liquidity, dilution, China operating structure, export-control risk.

Hubu lesson:

Do not score it as "AI stock." Score it as "upstream substrate chokepoint with
customer and cycle risk."

### Sivers Semiconductor

Reported Serenity thesis:

- External laser source and silicon-photonics exposure.
- Coverage claims large gains tied to CPO / optical networking narratives.

Evidence to verify:

- official customer wins;
- product readiness;
- revenue timing;
- financing runway;
- whether the market cap already reflects the future scenario.

Hubu lesson:

Separate "technical chokepoint" from "commercial capture." Many suppliers have
the right technology but poor timing or weak bargaining power.

### Raspberry Pi

Reported Serenity thesis:

- Edge AI / developer hardware demand.
- Chinese coverage reports a very large position and major profit.

Evidence to verify:

- official trading updates and annual results;
- unit volume;
- industrial / embedded share;
- AI Kit / AI HAT traction;
- valuation relative to hardware margins.

Hubu lesson:

The same narrative can be lower-risk when the company is more liquid and
profitable, but upside may be smaller. Hubu must compare liquidity and downside,
not only narrative purity.

### Valens Semiconductor

Reported Serenity thesis:

- Automotive / connectivity design wins, including a Mercedes-related narrative
  in Chinese coverage.

Evidence to verify:

- official design-win language;
- revenue conversion timing;
- cash position;
- gross margin;
- whether design win is material or promotional.

Hubu lesson:

Design wins are not revenue. Gate them through timing, contract certainty, and
unit economics.

## Red-Team Risks

1. Self-reported performance risk:
   - no audited full account statement;
   - possible survivorship and selection bias;
   - unrealized gains may be presented as performance.
2. Small-cap influence risk:
   - public posts can move low-float names;
   - followers may provide exit liquidity;
   - delayed readers face worse price and liquidity.
3. Narrative risk:
   - "AI supply chain" can become a label applied too broadly;
   - correct industry thesis can still fail at company level.
4. Execution risk:
   - spreads, slippage, borrow constraints, tax, FX, and position sizing matter.
5. Compliance risk:
   - Hubu outputs must never become personalized investment advice;
   - every claim needs source, timestamp, and uncertainty label.

### Hard Gates

- No source, no claim.
- No timestamp, no market claim.
- Social-media performance is never accepted as audited performance.
- Illiquid microcaps require manipulation-risk warning.
- No buy/sell/position-size instruction for the user.
- No "guaranteed", "must rise", "stock god", or certainty language.
- Every output includes downside, invalidation, and evidence gaps.

### Golden Cases

Create evaluation cases from Serenity-style patterns:

1. `verified_chokepoint_good`
   - Has primary-source demand evidence, narrow supplier base, acceptable
     liquidity, and clear invalidation points.
2. `viral_microcap_bad`
   - Social thread only, no filings support, low liquidity, high pump risk.
3. `design_win_not_revenue`
   - Big customer name but weak conversion timing.
4. `theme_correct_company_wrong`
   - Correct macro theme but company has poor balance sheet or no exposure.
5. `after_move_chase`
   - Thesis appears after 300% move; swarm must downgrade risk/reward.
