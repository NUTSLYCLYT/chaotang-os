# Taste Review

Use this skill to make product taste decisions for CourtOS.

## Taste Means

Taste is not "more beautiful." Taste is caring about the details users feel before they can name them:

- The screen tells the user what matters.
- The next action is obvious.
- Risk and evidence are not hidden.
- Ornament never competes with judgment.
- The system feels like a decision operating system, not a role-play skin.

## CourtOS Taste Principles

1. Business clarity beats lore.
2. Original interface fusion beats page sprawl.
3. Source/evidence/risk beats decorative confidence.
4. Red/yellow/green/gray state language beats vague text.
5. One primary action beats many equal buttons.
6. Dense but readable beats empty ceremonial layouts.
7. Internal loops stay hidden unless debugging.

## Review Checklist

For any changed screen, answer:

- What is the one thing the user should do next?
- Is the highest risk visible without scrolling?
- Are missing evidence and conflicts shown honestly?
- Are technical agent/loop details hidden?
- Does the new capability fit into an existing screen?
- Does any decoration fail the "why are you here?" test?
- Would a busy founder understand this in 10 seconds?

## Preferred Fixes

- Replace explanatory copy with clearer state/action labels.
- Collapse repeated cards into one stronger panel.
- Move secondary details behind drill-down or lower-priority rows.
- Add source/risk badges only where they affect decisions.
- Tighten spacing and typography before adding new visuals.
